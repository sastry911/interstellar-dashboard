WHY CLOCKS DISAGREE

Two effects, one universe

Gravitational time dilationGravity / geometry

A clock deeper in a gravitational field can tick more slowly relative to a clock farther away, under a specified comparison.

Velocity time dilationRelative motion

In special relativity, a moving clock accumulates less proper time between suitable reunion events than an inertial reference clock.

There is no universal “now” shared by all observers. Exact comparisons depend on paths through spacetime and how clocks are compared.
REFERENCE EQUATION

Special-relativistic time dilation

Δt = γ Δτ · γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²)

For an inertial clock moving at speed v relative to an inertial frame, γ relates coordinate time Δt to the clock's proper time Δτ. This formula alone does not model Miller's orbit.

Gargantua · rotating black hole

ClassKerr black hole concept
Key featureAngular momentum
Event horizonBoundary of no return

A black hole itself emits no light from inside its event horizon; glowing material around it can radiate intensely.

01 · EVENT HORIZON

Not a solid surface

The event horizon is a causal boundary: once inside, future-directed paths cannot reach distant outside observers. Crossing it need not be locally dramatic for a sufficiently large black hole.

02 · SINGULARITY

Where theory breaks down

Classical general relativity predicts singularities in idealized black-hole solutions. A complete quantum-gravity description remains an open problem.

03 · TIDAL FORCES

Spaghettification

Gravity's gradient can stretch and compress an object. Tidal forces at the horizon can be smaller for a supermassive black hole than for a small one.

WHY ROTATION MATTERS

Kerr spacetime and frame dragging

A rotating black hole drags nearby spacetime around with it. Spin changes the horizon structure and possible orbits. Extreme time dilation on a stable, habitable planet requires particular orbital and black-hole parameters; it is not a generic outcome.

Schwarzschild radius (non-rotating idealization): rₛ = 2GM / c²

This radius formula is for a non-rotating, uncharged black hole. Gargantua is portrayed as rapidly rotating, so the full Kerr geometry is more relevant.

Science vs. cinematic fiction

The film mixes established physics, informed extrapolation, and deliberate science fiction.

What is a bootstrap paradox?

A causal loop in which information or an object exists in a closed chain of events without a clear origin point. Cooper is both receiver and sender of the signals that guide his mission. This is a narrative time-loop model, not evidence that real-world messages can be sent into the past.
